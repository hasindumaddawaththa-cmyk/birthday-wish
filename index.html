May 23rd · Her 19th Birthday

To my Wassi Patiyo,

Happy birthday, my love. Today, the world gets to celebrate the day you were born — but honestly, I've been celebrating you every single day since you walked into my life.

"You support me through everything without ever being asked. That kind of love is rare, and I don't take it for granted for a single second."

I love the way you're sometimes wonderfully, adorably childish — it reminds me that you let me see the real you, the soft and playful parts that you don't show everyone. Those are my favourite parts.

The way you understand me, even when I don't fully understand myself — it's one of the most beautiful gifts anyone has ever given me. You see me. And still, you stay. Still, you want me. That means everything.

"I love everything you are. Not pieces of you — all of you, literally every part."

1,531 days together, and somehow it still feels like we're only just beginning. I don't know what the next year holds, but I know I want every single day of it to be with you. Here's to 19, my love — and to forever after that.

Forever yours 🤍
